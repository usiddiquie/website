I'm a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Bonn, working on robot learning at the Humanoid Robots Lab: safe reinforcement learning, imitation learning with flow-matching policies, and now world models.

My work covers the whole pipeline for learned robot policies: building simulation environments, training and benchmarking in them, and deploying on real robots with ROS2. I'm interested in learning-based control in general, and in how world models can make robots learn more from less data.
